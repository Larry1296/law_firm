# Kenyan Client Classification

Sheria Master classifies clients by legal capacity, not by industry.

The base `Client.client_type` identifies the person or legal entity instructing the firm:

- individual person
- sole proprietorship, represented by the proprietor trading under a business name
- company
- partnership
- limited liability partnership
- cooperative society, including SACCOs as a cooperative subtype
- society or association
- non-profit organization, with the underlying legal form recorded separately where relevant
- trust
- estate, acting through a lawful personal representative
- public entity
- international or intergovernmental organization

Sectors such as financial services, education, religion, and cooperative savings are not standalone legal capacities. They belong on the relevant profile as sector, purpose, subtype, registration status, or regulator information.

Representatives are separate records. A director, trustee, executor, administrator, partner, public officer, county attorney, or other authorized person is not stored as the client unless that person personally instructs the firm.

This system records capacity and authority information for advocate review. It does not make final legal determinations on capacity, joinder, authority to instruct, limitation, jurisdiction, immunity, or party naming.
